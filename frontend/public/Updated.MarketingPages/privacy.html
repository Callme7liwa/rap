Privacy Policy

How Realti Intel collects, uses, and protects your information.

Who We Are

Realti Intel is operated by Realti Intel Inc., a Massachusetts company. We provide a real estate intelligence platform built for licensed real estate agents in Massachusetts. Our platform delivers live MLS data, market scoring, and client-ready insights to help agents price, position, and advise with precision.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@realtiintel.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically when you use our site.

Information you provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Primary real estate market (city or town)
  • How you heard about Realti Intel (optional)

Information collected automatically:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and device type
  • Referring website or source
  • Clicks, scrolls, and general interaction patterns
  • IP address (used for geographic analytics only, not stored long-term)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your waitlist request and confirm your founding member status
  • Notify you when your requested market becomes available
  • Send onboarding and product update emails
  • Understand how people use our site so we can improve it
  • Communicate pricing, feature, and launch updates

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising on other platforms.

Email Communications

When you submit your information through our waitlist form, you are opting in to receive emails from Realti Intel. These emails include your confirmation, market availability updates, onboarding information, and occasional product news.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. Transactional emails (such as account or billing confirmations) cannot be unsubscribed from while you are an active subscriber.

We use ConvertKit to manage our email list. Your name and email address are stored in ConvertKit's systems. You can review ConvertKit's privacy policy at convertkit.com/privacy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymized usage data including page views, session duration, traffic sources, and general interaction patterns.

Microsoft Clarity may record session replays and generate heatmaps of how visitors interact with our pages. This data is anonymized and used only to improve our site experience. No personally identifiable information is captured through session recordings.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can opt out of Microsoft Clarity at clarity.microsoft.com/optout.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to support analytics and to remember your preferences (such as whether you have dismissed a notification). We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect how some features of the site function.

Data Storage and Security

Our website is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure in the United States. Your data is transmitted over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or update your information
  • Request that we delete your information from our systems
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@realtiintel.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Realti Intel is a professional tool intended for licensed real estate agents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify active users by email. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at info@realtiintel.com.
