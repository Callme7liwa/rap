Questions we get.

What does "your market going live" mean?
Realti Intel is launching region by region across Massachusetts. If your primary market isn't live yet, we'll notify you the moment it is. Your founding rate locks at confirmation. Billing starts when your market goes live, so you're never charged for a platform you can't use.
Can I add additional towns after signing up?
Founding Member includes your primary market. If you work across multiple towns, the Agent Pro and Team tiers are built for broader coverage. Founding members also get priority access as new markets open, so if your adjacent towns aren't live yet, you'll be first in.
How is this different from RPR, ShowingTime, or my broker's tools?
Those tools give you access to superficial level data. Realti Intel gives you actionable intelligence, scored, weighted, and interpreted so you know exactly what the numbers mean and what to do with them. RPR doesn't tell you whether to price at $749K or $779K, or what it costs your seller if you're wrong. ShowingTime doesn't tell you the optimal week to list for this property type in this town. Your broker's tools don't go deeper than whatever their vendor built years ago. Realti Intel gives you the knowledge to price with precision, negotiate from a position of strength, and see market shifts before your competition does.
Is this for buyer agents, listing agents, or both?
Both. Seasonal Intelligence and Market Opportunities are particularly valuable for buyer agents. MPS, Sold Analytics, and CMA Generator are built for listing presentations. Most agents find they use all ten modules across both sides of a transaction.
What if my market isn't covered yet?
Tell us where you work when you join the waitlist. We prioritize new markets based on demand concentration. The more agents who request a specific town, the faster we build it out.
